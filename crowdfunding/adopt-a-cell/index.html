It comes apart into modules

Take the container away and the pack splits into modules. Each one stays under 120 volts, so it can be built and serviced safely on the bench.

Inside a module

Every module is a block of cylindrical lithium-ion cells, each a little bigger than an AA battery, joined into groups by busbars. These cells are what your £5 buys.
